# Flow: project lifecycle

Every project moves along the stage bar shown on its detail page:

`Prospect → Proposal In Progress → Risk Approvals → Proposal Doc Reviews → Submit to Client → Won/Lost/Not Going Ahead`

- Stage can be changed from the **Stage** control in the project header.
- At **Risk Approvals**, items should appear in `/approve-risks` (Risk Approval tab).
- At **Proposal Doc Reviews**, documents should appear in `/reviews`
  (Technical Review / Proposal Director Review tabs) and in the project's own `reviews` tab.
- Won projects show status **Won** in the projects list and surface in **PM Hub** with
  contract value, invoiced, WIP and budget remaining.

**Unverified:** who may move a stage, what gates each transition, and whether the queues
populate automatically. This is the highest-value flow to confirm with a QA engineer.
