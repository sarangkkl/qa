# Notifications

**Panel:** clicking **Notifications** in the left nav opens an in-page panel.
Empty state: "All caught up! You have no unread notifications" plus a
**See all notifications** link.

**Page:** `/notifications`
- Filters: **All**, **Unread**, **Read**
- Sort: **Newest First**
- Empty state: "No notifications found — You don't have any notifications matching your
  current filters."
