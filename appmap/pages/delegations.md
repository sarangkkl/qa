# Delegations

**Route:** `/delegations` (reached from **Manage delegations** on `/approve-risks`)

Heading: **Approver Responsibility Management**, with a section **Approval Delegated to Me**
("…risk parameters have been delegated to you").

Lets an approver hand their risk-approval responsibility to someone else. The delegation
mechanics (who can delegate, to whom, for how long) are **not yet mapped** — no controls
were visible on the account used for mapping, which may itself be permission-dependent.
